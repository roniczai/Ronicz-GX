# Why RONICZ GX matters

**Vision:** A world in which capable personal computing belongs on ordinary desks, not only in well-funded companies or remote data centres.

## The access problem we want to investigate

Gaming, rendering, local AI, video, 3D engineering and software development can depend on graphics hardware and memory that are difficult for many individuals and small teams to obtain, replace or upgrade. Availability and affordability vary across countries and use cases. This project makes **no numerical claim** about how many people are excluded.

Powerful tools can change what individuals are able to create. RONICZ GX is a mission to **research** lower-barrier, serviceable, multi-purpose personal computing.

## Who we design for first

1. **Students and self-taught builders** working locally without a dedicated lab.
2. **Gamers** seeking real graphics support, stability and sustained performance.
3. **Independent creators** using 3D, rendering, video, CAD and design software.
4. **Local AI developers and hobbyists** who benefit from dedicated VRAM and accessible tools.
5. **Small businesses and operators** who need owned, maintainable computing infrastructure.

Enterprise buyers are welcome, but **consumer needs set the design priorities**.

## How this mission affects design

- **Hardware autonomy:** local performance, dedicated GPU memory and separate upgradeable desktop RAM.
- **Maintainability:** prioritize accessible cooling, usable diagnostics and replaceable components where technically feasible.
- **Open research:** publish independent experiments, limitations, reproducible tests and documented trade-offs.
- **Practical compatibility:** target mainstream PCs and software over artificial vendor lock-in.
- **Efficiency:** measure real application performance per watt, at sustained load and documented ambient conditions.
- **Responsible communication:** do not advertise unreleased capabilities, savings, benchmark results or social outcomes.

## How we would measure impact, if hardware becomes real

- Independently measured consumer workload capability, stability and power use.
- Price and total ownership cost **only after** engineering and supply-chain validation.
- Customer ability to service, replace parts and continue using the system.
- Application compatibility across tested games, creator tools and AI frameworks.
- Accessibility of documentation, learning materials and dev tooling.
- User studies with students, creators and small teams, rather than generic testimonials.

## What we are not claiming

This is not yet a nonprofit programme, public-benefit certification, proven low-cost manufacturing method or revolutionary new GPU architecture. It is a **socially motivated engineering research initiative** seeking evidence that its goals are possible.
