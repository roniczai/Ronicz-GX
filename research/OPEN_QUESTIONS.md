# Open engineering questions

RONICZ GX is strongest when a reviewer can see exactly what we **do not yet know**.

## GPU architecture

1. Which execution model is attainable by a small research team: SIMD, SIMT, vector or a hybrid?
2. What is the smallest scheduler + memory hierarchy that can run a nontrivial reproducible compute kernel?
3. What is the efficiency trade-off between conventional raster hardware and pure compute-first approaches?
4. What reusable open, permissively licensed building blocks can legally shorten the research phase?

## Software and compatibility

5. What would it take to compile and run independent graphics shaders?
6. Which open rendering / compute APIs are realistic research entry points?
7. How would a Windows 11 WDDM path eventually work, and what team skill does it require?
8. Can the same hardware serve mainstream gaming and local AI without unacceptable compromises?

## Consumer access

9. How much dedicated GPU memory do actual students, creators and gamers need by workload?
10. Can cooling components be serviceable without compromising acoustics and stability?
11. Can standard motherboard/DDR5/NVMe parts make the GX Station easier to repair and upgrade?
12. What would *measurable* affordability and accessibility mean to different users and regions?

## Hardware feasibility

13. Which memory-chip densities and controller IP could support 12 / 24 / 48 GB goals?
14. What can FPGA experiments prove, and what cannot be extrapolated to ASIC?
15. What legitimate supply-chain and silicon-design route could ever lead to a marketable graphics card?

These questions are not specifications. Answers require tests and primary-source research, not marketing claims.
