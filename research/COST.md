# Cost and feasibility: ₹250,000 high-end target

**Target is hypothetical. No supplier quotes or ASIC business case have been obtained.**

## Price reference caveat

The user's cited street price of ₹500,000 for some RTX 5090 listings is not a universal benchmark. NVIDIA India marketplace has also listed the RTX 5090 at ₹239,000 as an official reference price (availability may vary). Therefore ₹250,000 cannot be advertised as a verified **50% discount** compared with the 5090.

References: https://marketplace.nvidia.com/en-in/consumer/graphics-cards/?gpu=RTX+5090

## Real cost model

Consumer retail price must cover, at minimum:

- GPU die cost, wafer yield, packaging and test.
- GDDR memory devices and associated controller / PHY IP license.
- PCB layers, stack-up, high-speed SI/PI engineering and fabrication.
- Voltage regulation, connectors, cooling hardware, heatsink, fans and enclosure.
- Final assembly, test, scrap, certification and import duties.
- Engineering salaries, verification, silicon EDA and prototype iteration.
- Software, driver development, security updates, warranty, support and returns.
- Distribution, taxes, channel margins and foreign-exchange exposure.

A gross margin is not net profit. Do not calculate profitability by subtracting only RAM and PCB BOM.

## Feasibility questions before a price announcement

1. Are we designing original silicon or assembling a card based on a licensed GPU?
2. How much nonrecurring engineering expense is required?
3. Which foundry, process node, package, memory interface and IP rights are feasible?
4. What are the minimum order quantities and annual production volumes?
5. Which compliance tests, drivers and applications must be supported at launch?
6. What yield assumptions, exchange rates and warranty reserves apply?
7. Can tested performance per watt and actual buyer demand justify the product?

## Decision

Continue research; **do not** accept preorders, quote a retail price, promise RTX 5090 performance, or claim 50% cost savings. Price remains a **design constraint**, not a demonstrated outcome.
