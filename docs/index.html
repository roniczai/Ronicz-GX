01 / WHY WE'RE HERE

The next generation
should get to build.

OUR PRINCIPLE
Access / not exclusivity

We are pursuing a socially motivated technical question: can capable personal computing become easier to own, maintain and use? There is no demonstrated product or affordability claim yet.
